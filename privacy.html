Privacy Policy

Effective 29 June 2026

This privacy policy applies to the FamilyMeals app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by FamilyMeals (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

To provide the service, the Application collects and stores the following:

  • Account information — the email address your sign-in provider shares with us (via Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In), plus a display name or nickname that you choose. If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only receive Apple's private-relay address. Members who join an existing household with an invite code use an anonymous sign-in and do not provide an email.
  • Household information you enter — a household name, a timezone, and the names or nicknames of the members in your household.
  • Meal-planning content you enter — meals and their ingredients, your weekly meal plan, who is cooking each meal, votes and voting rounds, shopping-list items, and roulette scenarios, options and spin history.
  • Subscription status — whether your household has an active paid plan.
  • An Apple or Google refresh token — stored only so we can revoke your Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In credential when you delete your account.
  • On your device only — your sign-in session and, on a shared household device, a small cache of which member is currently active.

This information is used solely to operate the Application — to authenticate you, sync your household's meal-planning data across devices, and manage your plan. We do not use it for advertising, analytics, or marketing.

Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

No. The Application does not collect any location information.

Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

Yes — the Application relies on the following service providers to operate. No other third parties receive your information.

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple. Used for authentication. Apple shares your email (real or private-relay) with us. Privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google — Google Sign-In (authentication) and Google Play billing. Google shares your email and name with us when you sign in; you can set a different display name inside the app. Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.). Hosts the database and authentication. All account, household, and meal-planning data listed above is stored here. Privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.). Manages the optional subscription. Receives a random household identifier and the App Store or Google Play transaction. No name or email is sent. Privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Payment is processed by Apple via the App Store, or by Google via Google Play; we never see your card details. The Application does not include any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not collect advertising identifiers, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or any device sensor data.

What are my opt-out rights?

The household owner can delete the account from within the Application (Settings → Delete account), which removes the household and all associated members, meals, ingredients, meal plan, votes, shopping-list items and roulette data, and revokes the stored Apple or Google refresh token. You can also uninstall the Application at any time using the standard uninstall process on your device.

Children

The Application is a general-audience meal-planning tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Members are added to a household by name or nickname only; the Application does not ask members to provide their own personal information.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) between the Application and our service providers, and is stored on Supabase's secured infrastructure with row-level access controls that restrict each household's data to its own members. No system is perfectly secure, but the Service Provider works to protect your information using industry-standard practices.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. The Service Provider will notify you of any changes to their Privacy Policy by updating this page with the new Privacy Policy. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.

This privacy policy is effective as of 2026-06-29.

Your Consent

By using the Application, you are consenting to the processing of your information as set forth in this Privacy Policy now and as amended by the Service Provider.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, or have questions about the practices, please contact the Service Provider via email at support@familymeals.co.nz.
